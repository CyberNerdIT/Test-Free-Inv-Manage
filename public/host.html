Tech Garage

Tech Garage is a free, open-source, self-hosted system for buying, refurbishing and reselling computers — with cost & break-even tracking, profit charts, eBay & Amazon price comparison, and a private, invite-only customer storefront. Everything runs on your own server; no data leaves it.

🧮

Cost & break-even tracking

Log what you paid plus every repair cost, and see the exact price you need to turn a profit — fees and shipping included.

📈

Profit over time

Dashboard charts for realized and projected profit, ROI, sell-through rate and average days-to-sell.

🔍

eBay & Amazon comps

Compare against active listings and recently-sold prices to nail your pricing before you list.

🛒

Private storefront

Invite customers to a clean storefront that shows only your listing prices — never your costs or profit — with a cart, offers and purchase requests.

🔔

Alerts & notifications

Get pinged on new purchase requests via email, WhatsApp, Discord or Telegram; customers can subscribe to stock alerts.

🔐

Yours, self-hosted

Zero-dependency Node.js + SQLite. One-line install, your branding, your server. MIT licensed — fork it and make it your own.
